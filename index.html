Questions answered

Why do normal AI picks make customers walk away?

Standard language models rank choices by internet fame. When you ask for a DJ, a sponsor, or a creator, they pick 98%-famous names that belong to a completely different cultural crowd, breaking the room's vibe immediately.

How does Qloo know what fits without personal tracking?

Qloo maps anonymous cultural correlations across 250 million entities spanning music, film, TV, dining, books, podcasts, places, and brands, using zero personally identifiable information.

What does the second verification check prove?

Replacing a bad pick is not enough until the replacement is tested. Blindspot runs the exact same Qloo affinity, tag-overlap, and demographic checks on the new plan to prove it scores 5 out of 5 before generating your outreach kit.
